KRUNKER 1.5.6

1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere (not inside the zip).
2. Double-click Krunker.exe.
   Windows might say "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".

On the start screen:
- PLAY finds a game in the mode you picked, or starts one that others join.
- To play with a friend: they press PLAY (or host), a code shows at the top of their menu, they send it to you,
  you paste it in "Join a friend" and press JOIN.
- Have Steam open to play with people over the internet. (Steam will say you're playing "Spacewar". That's normal.)

Your account, KR and skins are kept by whoever hosts the game.
